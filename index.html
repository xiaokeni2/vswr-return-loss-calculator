Formulas

  • Γ = (VSWR − 1) / (VSWR + 1)
  • Return Loss (dB) = −20·log₁₀(|Γ|)
  • Mismatch Loss (dB) = −10·log₁₀(1 − |Γ|²)
  • VSWR = (1 + |Γ|) / (1 − |Γ|)
  • Reflected power (%) = |Γ|² × 100

Return loss is the ratio of incident to reflected power at a port, expressed in dB. Mismatch loss is the fraction of available power lost due to the impedance mismatch — at VSWR 2.0 about 11% of forward power is reflected and 0.51 dB is lost.
